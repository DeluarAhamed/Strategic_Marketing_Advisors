Who we are

Strategic Marketing Advisors, LLC is a business and marketing consultancy built on real-world experience — Fortune 500 boardrooms, the U.S. Air Force, the university lecture hall, and two small businesses of our own.

Our story

Experience, not theory

Strategic Marketing Advisors was founded on a simple conviction: that the best marketing advice comes from people who have actually built things. We've sat in Fortune 500 boardrooms. We've launched startups from nothing. We've taught at the university level and coached executives through their hardest decisions.

We specialize in helping companies of all sizes get and keep more profitable customers using high-impact, low-cost Internet and offline marketing strategies. We don't believe in trendy tactics or one-size-fits-all playbooks — we understand your business deeply, find what actually moves the needle, and execute with discipline.

  • Veteran-owned — 20 years of U.S. Air Force service
  • Authors of three Entrepreneur Press books
  • Certified Guerrilla Marketing Coaches

Core philosophies

Principles we won't compromise

The same standards we held in command and in the boardroom guide every engagement.

Passion first

We only recommend strategies we've tested ourselves — in real boardrooms and real startups, with real money on the line.

Transfer, don't trap

We build your team's capability instead of your dependence on us. When we leave, you own the playbook and the results.

Measure what matters

Vanity metrics are worthless. We track revenue impact, lead quality, acquisition cost, and lifetime value — nothing else.

Accountability

We own our work. If a strategy isn't producing, we say so, we fix it, and we tell you the truth about why.

Disciplined execution

Twenty years of military command translates directly into precise, on-time, on-budget delivery.

High impact, low cost

You don't need a massive budget to win. You need the right approach — and that's exactly what we bring.

Leadership

Meet Mary & David

A husband-and-wife team, authors, coaches, and consultants — with two decades of Fortune 500 and U.S. Air Force experience between them.

Mary Eule Scarborough
Award-winning speaker, writer & Certified Guerrilla Marketing Coach

Mary weaves humor throughout her practical, plain-speaking advice, drawing on real-life experience as a Fortune 500 marketing executive, founder of two successful small businesses, and independent strategic marketing consultant. She and David co-authored The Procrastinator's Guide to Marketing, and Mary also co-authored Mastering Online Marketing and Guerrilla Marketing on the Internet (Entrepreneur Press). She holds a degree in Journalism & English from the University of Maryland and a master's in marketing from The Johns Hopkins University.

David A. Scarborough
Business leader, educator, writer & speaker

David applies his expertise in business, finance, economics, and strategic marketing to help entrepreneurs grow and maintain healthy businesses. He served as a U.S. Air Force officer and aviator for 20 years before retiring, and taught business courses at Charleston Southern University. A certified Guerrilla Marketing coach and co-author of The Procrastinator's Guide to Marketing, David holds a bachelor's in aviation from Western Michigan University and an MBA from Golden Gate University — and remains a certified commercial and instructor pilot.

The Scarboroughs

Their life together

David and Mary split their time between summer and winter homes in the mountains of western North Carolina. They feel fortunate to have built a thriving business that lets them do what they love — sharing their passion for marketing with readers, seminar students, and conference audiences.

It also leaves room for what matters most: their individual and shared hobbies, and time with each other, their children, grandchildren, extended family, and friends.
