Our signature services

What is an AI Voice Agent?

An AI Voice Agent acts as your AI front desk: answering routine calls, helping with appointment booking and supporting lead follow-up. We define the information, rules and human handoffs around your business before rollout.

Can I try it for my own business?

Start with SMA’s live website demo to hear the experience. Then book a tailored walkthrough where we discuss your own services, appointment rules and customer scenarios. The website demo does not automatically build a custom agent from your website.

What is VedaKosa?

VedaKosa is our hands-on AI business system engagement. We work with you to organize business knowledge, build useful workflows and help your team adopt the system. Tools, connections and support are agreed in your scope.

How many VedaKosa clients are you looking to work with?

Our current goal is to build VedaKosa systems with about 10 businesses so the work can stay focused and hands-on. A conversation helps us assess fit; this is a program focus rather than a claim about remaining spaces.

Can I buy SEO or AEO as a standalone service?

Our AI Visibility work—including SEO, answer engine optimization and local search—is available to clients using our AI Voice Agent or VedaKosa services. We are not currently promoting it as a one-off engagement.

Where can I find free DIY resources?

LocalPromoVault provides free marketing resources, a magazine and a newsletter. Our free The Next Level Skool community adds practical learning and support. You do not need to be a client to use them.

About SMA

What is Strategic Marketing Advisors?

We’re a veteran-owned business and marketing consultancy helping companies get and keep more profitable customers through high-impact, low-cost strategies. Today our signature services are AI Voice Agents and VedaKosa. Our experience includes Fortune 500 marketing, 23 years of Air Force service and businesses we started ourselves.

Who runs the firm?

A husband-and-wife team, Mary and David Scarborough. Mary is a former Fortune 500 marketing executive, founder of two small businesses and Certified Guerrilla Marketing Coach with a master’s in marketing from Johns Hopkins. David served 23 years as a U.S. Air Force officer and aviator, taught business at Charleston Southern University and holds an MBA from Golden Gate University. Their published work includes three Entrepreneur Press books.

What does “veteran-owned” actually mean for me as a client?

David’s 23 years as an Air Force officer and aviator inform how we work: define the objective, build the plan and follow through. You get straight talk, honest counsel and clear responsibility throughout the engagement.

What do you mean by “experience, not theory”?

We ground our advice in practical experience in businesses, marketing and leadership. We help you apply a plan to the way your business works, rather than leaving you with a strategy deck and no next step.

What are the principles behind your work?

Use practical methods; transfer skills instead of creating dependency; measure what matters to the business; own the work; execute with discipline; and look for high impact without unnecessary expense.

How we work

What does a typical engagement look like?

Discover your business and numbers, diagnose the constraints, build a focused strategy, execute the agreed work, review progress and transfer the playbook and skills to your team. The exact scope and review schedule are set in your proposal.

Will my team learn to run this ourselves?

Yes. Knowledge transfer is part of the approach. We help your team understand the system, its responsibilities and how to maintain the agreed workflows, with ongoing support defined in the engagement.

What metrics do you track?

Useful business measures such as revenue impact, lead quality, customer acquisition cost and lifetime value. For an AI front desk or workflow we also agree relevant operating measures, such as answered inquiries, booking handoffs and follow-up completion.

What size businesses do you work with?

From funded startups to established mid-market companies. The best fit is an owner or leadership team with a clear business need, a workable budget and a willingness to make decisions and adopt the system.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. We are based in Cashiers, North Carolina and work with clients across the country remotely. David and Mary live full time in the mountains of western North Carolina. Meetings are Monday–Friday; Saturday and Sunday are closed.

Consulting & engagement

What is executive business consulting with SMA?

We work with leadership to clarify strategy, positioning and decisions. That consulting experience supports the way we plan and implement AI Voice Agents and VedaKosa: a practical advisor who understands both the business goal and the work needed to get there.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. We earn the relationship through the work. Your proposal sets out the scope, fees, review points and any support or cancellation terms so you can understand the commitment before starting.

What makes your consulting different from a strategy deck?

AI Visibility: SEO, AEO & local search

How is SEO different from paid traffic?

Paid traffic depends on ongoing advertising spend. SEO improves the information and technical foundations that help people discover your site organically. It takes time and continued attention, and results vary. Our SEO work supports clients using our two signature services.

What is included in your SEO work?

Depending on the agreed client scope: technical site health, crawlability, service-page content, internal links, accurate local business information and trust signals such as a useful review-response process. AEO adds clear answers and information that is easier for search and AI-assisted systems to interpret.

How long does SEO take to show results?

There is no fixed timeline. Search engines need time to recrawl changes, and meaningful progress can take months depending on the starting point and competition. We review evidence over time rather than guarantee a ranking or AI answer placement.

AI software & automation

What is AI software and automation for my business?

In VedaKosa, we focus on the repetitive work and information handoffs that are worth improving in your own business. This can include agreed CRM and follow-up workflows. It is part of the system we build with you, rather than a separate menu of tools.

Do I need technical knowledge to use these tools?

No. We help choose a usable approach, handle the agreed setup and show your team how it works. You stay involved in the decisions, information and approvals that matter.

Will AI replace the human touch in my marketing?

The goal is to give routine tasks a reliable first response while keeping people involved where judgment or personal attention is needed. You define the agent’s boundaries, escalation rules and review process with us.

How do you decide what to automate?

We map the workflow, identify repetitive tasks and choose a focused use case with a useful business benefit. We also plan the exceptions: what happens when the information is missing or a person needs to step in.
