# Strategic Marketing Advisors (SMA)

> Strategic Marketing Advisors, LLC is a veteran-owned, founder-led consultancy based in Cashiers, North Carolina, serving businesses remotely nationwide. SMA helps home-service business owners answer customer calls, follow up on opportunities and put practical AI to work. Its signature services are AI Voice Agents and VedaKosa. AI Visibility is also available independently.

## Services

- [AI Voice Agents]({{SITE_URL}}/services/ai-voice-agents): an AI front desk configured around a business's services, approved questions, appointment rules and human handoffs. Helps answer calls, capture lead details and support booking and follow-up through the agreed integrations.
- [Talk with Kate in the voice demo]({{SITE_URL}}/demo): Kate is SMA's AI assistant and demo, not the name of the voice-agent product. Voice loads only after visitor consent, and microphone access is needed only when the visitor chooses to speak.
- [VedaKosa Private AI Assistant]({{SITE_URL}}/services/vedakosa): a private AI assistant on its own computer, working 24/7 with authorized email, calendar, commitments and connected tools. It remembers business context. Data ownership, permissions, integrations and action approvals are agreed during setup; connections to other services are reviewed with the client.
- [AI Visibility Assessment and Programs]({{SITE_URL}}/services/ai-visibility): start with a free assessment and prioritized 30-day roadmap. Choose DIY, a 30-day done-for-you engagement or an extended 90-day program. No AI Voice Agent or VedaKosa purchase is required. Search rankings and AI recommendations are not guaranteed.
- [Get the free AI Visibility Assessment](https://mybizreport.com/sma/aeo)
