Connect

Link your Google Business Profile and automatically import all of your reviews — including older ones you never got around to answering.

Reputation management FAQ

Everything you need to know about our AI-powered Google review platform.

It helps business owners effectively and efficiently manage their Google Business Profile reviews. It uses AI — specifically OpenAI's GPT-4 — to generate tailored responses to reviews, streamlining the process for busy owners.

It integrates directly with your Google Business Profile and automatically generates responses to the reviews you receive using GPT-4. Each response is then sent to you for approval, editing, or rejection.

GPT-4 is a state-of-the-art artificial intelligence model developed by OpenAI for tasks including translation, question-answering, and text generation. We use its ability to understand and generate human-like text to craft relevant responses to your reviews.

No. The platform is designed to be user-friendly and intuitive. You only need to review, edit, and approve the AI-generated responses.

Yes. You can edit, rewrite, or even reject any response the AI generates. The platform sends responses to you for review, so you have full control over what is finally posted to your Google Business Profile.

The AI is designed to learn and adapt over time. The more feedback and input you provide, the better it gets at understanding and matching your brand's voice and tone.

Yes. The platform generates responses for all reviews, both positive and negative. For negative reviews it focuses on addressing concerns, offering solutions, and demonstrating a commitment to customer satisfaction.

Typically within a few minutes of a new review arriving. The exact speed depends on factors such as the complexity of the review and the platform's current load.
